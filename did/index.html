Difference-in-Differences

Use changes in a comparison group to construct a counterfactual for a treated group—and learn when that comparison can support a causal interpretation.

What we study

Identify treated and comparison groups, distinguish correlation from causality, construct a counterfactual, calculate DiD, and connect the estimate to a regression. We also examine the parallel-trends assumption and threats to a causal interpretation.
